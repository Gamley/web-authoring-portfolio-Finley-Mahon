CYBERNETIC HEART

Cybernetic Heart is a song written from the perspective of a cyborg; one of the failed subjects of the Roots vaccine. The song follows them as they are forced to work for Gran against their will, but as was written in their contract, before attempting an unsuccessful rebellion and escape attempt and ends with them sending out an SOS.

Release Date: Jul 29, 2024
